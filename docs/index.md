<p class="ab-kicker">About</p>
<h2>Data science that solves real problems</h2>
<div class="ab-about">
<div class="ab-about__text">
<p>I'm a data scientist and a graduate of <strong>École Centrale Casablanca</strong>, where I studied data science and digitalization. Since June 2026 I have been a <strong>Data Scientist Confirmé at AXA Assurance Maroc</strong>, working on a machine-learning project to detect fraud in auto insurance.</p>
<p>Before AXA, I spent two and a half years building AI products end to end: a <strong>semantic search engine</strong> and <strong>manufacturing-time prediction models</strong> at Wineo Artificial Intelligence, then <strong>LLM agents, RAG chatbots and computer-vision applications</strong> at The Game Changer Company. Earlier internships in France and Morocco took me through deep learning for health data, object detection in operating rooms and industrial analytics.</p>
<p>What drives me is the same at every step: using data to unravel complex problems and make meaningful improvements in people's lives, with a strong belief in continuous learning and hard work.</p>

About

Data science that solves real problems

I'm a data scientist and a graduate of École Centrale Casablanca, where I studied data science and digitalization. Since June 2026 I have been a Data Scientist Confirmé at AXA Assurance Maroc, working on a machine-learning project to detect fraud in auto insurance.

Before AXA, I spent two and a half years building AI products end to end: a semantic search engine and manufacturing-time prediction models at Wineo Artificial Intelligence, then LLM agents, RAG chatbots and computer-vision applications at The Game Changer Company. Earlier internships in France and Morocco took me through deep learning for health data, object detection in operating rooms and industrial analytics.

What drives me is the same at every step: using data to unravel complex problems and make meaningful improvements in people's lives, with a strong belief in continuous learning and hard work.

What I work on

Fraud detection & machine learning

Machine learning applied to auto insurance fraud at AXA Assurance Maroc. Earlier: manufacturing-time prediction with one model per work centre, and classification with K-NN, CART and stacking ensembles.

Generative AI & LLM agents

Multi-step agents with LangGraph, RAG chatbots with LangChain and ChromaDB, semantic search, and local LLM stacks with Ollama and Open WebUI.

Computer vision

Object detection with YOLO, face and facial-attribute analysis, fine-tuned CNNs (VGG16, InceptionV3) and real-time people counting on the edge with NVIDIA Jetson Nano.

ML engineering & deployment

From notebook to production: FastAPI and Flask services, Docker, REST APIs, dashboards with Dash and Plotly, and deployments on OVH Cloud and on-premise servers.
